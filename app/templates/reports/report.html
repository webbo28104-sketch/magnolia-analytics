How to read this report
  • Strokes gained (SG) compares every shot with what a scratch (0 handicap) golfer averages from the same distance and lie. 0 = scratch. −0.30 means that shot cost you 0.3 strokes more than a scratch golfer; negative numbers are normal for anyone who isn’t scratch.
  • Plays like turns that into a handicap: strokes lost to scratch, scaled to 18 holes. Each part of the game counts for a quarter, so −2 off the tee over 9 holes is −4 per 18 and plays like a 16 handicap. On a breakdown row it means “if every shot like this were played this well, you’d play to a …”.
  • Per shot is the average across the shots in that group; Total is all of them added up. Category totals add up to your round total.
  • The confidence bars (▮▮▮▮) show how much data is behind a number: 1 bar = fewer than 3 shots, too few to judge; 4 bars = 30+ shots, solid. The ± range is where the true average probably sits; if the whole range is below 0, the loss is more than normal shot-to-shot luck.
  • Gold tick = your own average over your previous 10 rounds, so you can see whether today was normal for you.
  • Recurring means the same thing also cost you strokes in most of your recent rounds: worth practising. Unusual for you means it is normally fine: probably a bad day, not a pattern.
