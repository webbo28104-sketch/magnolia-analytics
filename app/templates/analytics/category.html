How to read this page
  • Strokes gained (SG) compares each {{ cfg.unit }} with what a Tour player averages from the same spot. +0.10 means that {{ cfg.unit }} was a tenth of a stroke better than Tour; −0.30 means it cost you about a third of a stroke. 0 = Tour level.
  • Per {{ cfg.unit }} is the average, so you can compare groups of different sizes. Total is the sum: how many strokes that group actually cost or gained over the period.
  • Confidence bars (▮▮▮▮) show how much data is behind a number: 1 bar = under 3 {{ cfg.unit }}s, too few to judge; 2 = early signal; 3 = developing (10+); 4 = solid (30+). The ± range is where your true average probably sits. If the whole range is below 0, the loss is more than normal shot-to-shot luck.
  • Lost strokes in X of Y rounds tells you whether something is a pattern (most rounds) or a one-off bad day.
  • Everything here uses your last {{ rounds_n }} complete rounds{% if prev_rounds_n %}, compared with the {{ prev_rounds_n }} before that{% endif %}{% if lie or wind %}, with the filters above applied{% endif %}.
