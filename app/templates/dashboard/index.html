How to read these numbers
  • Strokes gained (SG) compares every shot you log with what a PGA Tour pro averages from the same distance and lie. 0 = Tour average; negative is normal for every amateur. What matters is whether your numbers move towards 0 over time.
  • SG per round is scaled to 18 holes so 9-hole rounds compare fairly. SG per shot is the average across every measured shot.
  • Last {{ window }} = your most recent {{ window }} rounds; changes compare them with the {{ window }} before.
  • Confidence bars show how much data is behind a number. Patterns need several rounds before they mean much.
