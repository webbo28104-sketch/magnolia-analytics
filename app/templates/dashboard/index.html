How to read these numbers
  • Strokes gained (SG) compares every shot you log with what a scratch (0 handicap) golfer averages from the same distance and lie. 0 = scratch; negative is normal for anyone who isn’t scratch. What matters is whether your numbers move towards 0 over time.
  • Plays like turns strokes gained into a handicap: strokes lost to scratch per 18 holes. Each part of the game counts for a quarter, so losing 4 strokes off the tee over 18 holes means your tee shots play like a 16 handicap.
  • SG per round is scaled to 18 holes so 9-hole rounds compare fairly. SG per shot is the average across every measured shot.
  • Last {{ window }} = your most recent {{ window }} rounds; changes compare them with the {{ window }} before.
  • Confidence bars show how much data is behind a number. Patterns need several rounds before they mean much.
